9. Takeaways

  • Atlanta held its show rate year over year. 15.35% against 15.76% in July 2025, on 54% fewer registrations. Attendance quality did not degrade with the smaller budget.
  • Most of the registration shortfall is budget, not delivery. The comparable 2025 Atlanta event spent $98,856, close to double the current per market level. Efficiency is down about 17% in both markets, and that is the part that is ours to work on.
  • Session timing is a real lever. Atlanta ranged from 8.3% to 24.2% show rate across sessions on similar volumes, with the Saturday 1:30 PM Duluth session both largest and best attended. That spread is worth more than any creative change.
  • Norfolk cost per registration ran 1.5x Atlanta's at $76.29 against $51.22, on four venues against eight.
  • Two things close this report: Norfolk attendance for Virginia Beach and the Thursday Newport News session, and the buyers export for both markets.
