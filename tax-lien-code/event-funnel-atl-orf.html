9. Insights and Actions

  • Atlanta's attendance quality held, but it did not improve. 15.35% show rate against the 16.46%, 15.76% and 15.95% Atlanta ran across its three 2025 events, so this sits just under that band. Worth stating plainly: spend was 44% lower this time, $98,856 last July against $55,059 now, and a tighter budget concentrates delivery on the better audiences, so show rate had room to rise rather than hold flat. Action: the two levers that actually move show rate are registration timing and session scheduling, and both are covered below, rather than treating a flat rate as a result.
  • The registration shortfall is mostly budget, not delivery. The comparable 2025 Atlanta event spent $98,856, close to double the current per market level. Of the 1,248 registration gap, about 1,029 comes from the lower budget and about 219 from efficiency being 17% lower. Action: we are using the full allocation in every market still open, with Orlando stepped up to $2,700 a day for its final four days, and we are reading the 2,000 target against the budget level that originally produced it.
  • Attendance is driven by when someone registers, not by how many ads they saw. This answers the question raised on the July 22 call. Our retargeting ran at 4.4 times average frequency against 1.7 to 2.6 on prospecting, yet its show rate was no higher, so heavier exposure is not what lifts attendance. What moves it is proximity to the event, with final-week registrants attending at roughly twice the rate of the eight to twenty-one day window. Action: concentrating spend into the final week on the next three launches and strengthening the pre-event reminder path for early registrants, rather than pushing frequency higher.
  • Session timing and venue move attendance more than registration volume does. Atlanta ranged from 8.3% to 24.2% show rate across eight sessions on similar registration counts, a nearly threefold spread. The best was Saturday at 1:30 PM in Duluth, which was also the largest session, while the weakest was a Wednesday evening. Action: we are sharing the per session read so venue and daypart selection can use it on upcoming tours, and weighting budget toward the strongest slots where the calendar allows.
